# AzureAce Demo Script (Hackathon Presentation)

Follow this 3-minute flow to demonstrate AzureAce to judges and learners:

---

## 1. Introduction (30 seconds)
- **Problem**: Beginners studying for AZ-900 are overwhelmed by jargon, unverified question dumps, and disconnected resources.
- **Solution**: **AzureAce — AZ-900 AI Study Buddy**. An approachable, grounded study companion that breaks down complex cloud concepts using relatable real-world analogies, verified RAG sources, and targeted interactive practice questions.

---

## 2. Core Interactive Chat Journey (60 seconds)
1. **Explore Topics**: Click on the **Cloud Concepts** or **Azure Architecture and Services** pill in the sidebar.
2. **Suggested Prompt**: Click on *"Explain the Shared Responsibility Model"* or type: *"What is the difference between Availability Zones and Regions?"*.
3. **Inspect the Answer**:
   - Notice the structured explanation: Clear core definition, beginner-friendly terms.
   - Point out the **Relatable Analogy** (e.g., comparing cloud tenants to renting an apartment complex or local water tanks).
   - Point out the **Source Badge**: Click the verified citation showing *Official Microsoft Learn* or *User-Supplied Reference*.

---

## 3. Quick Action Chips (30 seconds)
1. Click **"Simplify"** on the latest message to see the concept re-framed for a 12-year-old.
2. Click **"Compare"** to contrast with related Azure alternatives.
3. Click **"Test Me"** to immediately spin up an original practice quiz question on that exact concept.

---

## 4. Practice Quiz & Misconception Correction (45 seconds)
1. Navigate to the **Practice Quiz** tab or launch from the chat.
2. Select an option (intentionally pick a common distractor).
3. Click **Submit Answer**:
   - The feedback reveals whether the answer was correct.
   - Highlights the **Misconception Correction**: explains *why* the wrong answer is tempting and clarifies the nuance.
   - Shows the mandatory disclaimer: *"Practice questions, not official exam questions."*

---

## 5. Exam Day Logistics (15 seconds)
1. Switch topic to **Exam Logistics (OnVUE)**.
2. Ask: *"Can I wear headphones during my online proctored exam?"*
3. The system answers strictly using the isolated Pearson OnVUE rules (headphones prohibited, clean desk policy) without corrupting cloud technical facts.
