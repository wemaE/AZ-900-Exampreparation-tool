# Vibe Coding Hackathon — AZ-900 Exam Prep Edition

**Source:** User-supplied “Vibe Coding Hackathon for #1Million Devs 2026” document

## Problem statements in the brief

1. **Practice & Assessment — Smart Quiz Builder:** Give instant feedback and clear explanations, organized by AZ-900 topic.
2. **Memory & Revision — Flashcard Coach:** Help learners revise terms and concepts, focusing on repeated mistakes.
3. **Planning — Personal Study Planner:** Build a plan based on exam date, available study time, and weak areas.
4. **Support — AI Study Buddy:** Explain abstract cloud concepts simply, using relatable African examples or local languages.
5. **Progress — Readiness Tracker:** Track practice scores by topic and show readiness.

## Recommended MVP focus

Implement the **AI Study Buddy** as the core product:
- Learner asks about an AZ-900 concept.
- The assistant retrieves grounded source material.
- It explains the idea in beginner-friendly language.
- It gives one relatable example.
- It asks one original follow-up question.
- It explains mistakes when the learner answers incorrectly.

A small “test me” action can support this journey. Do not build five disconnected products in the first pass.
