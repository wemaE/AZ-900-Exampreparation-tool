# Online-Proctored Exam Logistics — Transcribed Screenshot Notes

**Source type:** Exam logistics, not AZ-900 subject content  
**Verification status:** Transcribed from the supplied screenshots; policies can vary by exam/program and may change. Recheck the current booking and official OnVUE instructions before exam day.

> Keep this file in a separate `exam-logistics` RAG collection. Do not mix it into retrieval for Azure technical questions.

## Technology requirements shown in the screenshots

- Windows 10 or macOS 14 or higher.
- Working webcam, microphone, and speaker; no headphones or headsets unless the exam's rules explicitly permit an exception.
- One display screen only.
- Stable internet with at least 6 Mbps download and 2 Mbps upload.
- Ability to close all applications except OnVUE.
- Virtual machines and beta operating systems are prohibited.
- The screenshots list mobile phones, tablets, headphones, earbuds, styluses, watches, smart/connected recording or AI devices, secondary/touchscreen displays, VPNs, corporate networks, and public/shared networks as prohibited.
- Run and pass the system test on the same device and network planned for exam day.
- Restart the computer and avoid competing streaming or large downloads on the network.
- Check the specific exam policy for exceptions.

## Testing space shown in the screenshots

The desk should be empty except for the testing computer, pre-approved items/comfort aids, and a beverage in an unmarked container.

The screenshots say to remove items on, under, or within arm's reach of the desk, including electronics, books, notes, paper, pens, tissue boxes, makeup, handkerchiefs, food, smoking products, bags, wallets, coats, glasses cases, and weapons.

The room should be quiet and free from distractions. Clear whiteboards and note boards. The candidate must remain alone, and no one else may view the screen.

The screenshots list bathrooms, public spaces such as offices/libraries/coffee shops, and environments where the candidate is not fully dressed as prohibited spaces. Check current official policy for the specific exam.

## Identification shown in the screenshots

The screenshots require a valid government-issued photo ID whose name exactly matches the exam booking. Listed examples include:
- International passport.
- Plastic driver's licence.
- National, state, provincial, or EU ID card.
- Alien registration card/green card/permanent resident or visa card.
- Approved Aadhaar cards under the relevant policy.
- Non-U.S. military ID, including spouse/dependent.
- Japanese employee or student ID with a Japanese Health Insurance Eligibility Certificate.

The screenshots say paper IDs are accepted only in Algeria, Brazil, Cameroon, and the Dominican Republic.

For candidates under 18, the screenshots state that the minor must present their own valid ID and a parent/guardian must be present at check-in with ID and consent.

The screenshots list expired, digital, damaged, copied, privately issued, birth certificates, naturalization papers, Geneva Convention ID cards, Canadian health insurance cards, certain U.S. government/security IDs, and IDs from restricted locations as prohibited. Because eligibility varies, confirm the current official list rather than relying only on this transcription.

## Check-in and exam conduct shown in the screenshots

- Begin check-in 30 minutes before the appointment.
- Check-in includes technology checks, photos of the candidate and ID, and a 360-degree room scan.
- The screenshots warn that failure to meet requirements can prevent testing and lead to forfeiture of the exam fee.
- The session is recorded for security, quality, and training purposes.
- Follow all on-screen and proctor instructions.
- Do not cheat, let someone else take the exam, record/share the screen, leave camera view unless permitted, speak/read aloud unless instructed, or access a phone unless explicitly permitted by the proctor.
- Use in-exam chat to contact a proctor.
- The screenshots suggest closing and relaunching OnVUE from the downloads folder if the computer freezes/disconnects; follow current official instructions if this happens.

## Allowances shown in the screenshots

- A digital whiteboard is listed as available. This does not permit a physical whiteboard or writing materials.
- The screenshots include a special allowance for young children in the testing space, with instructions to inform the proctor when leaving camera view; nursing mothers are told to request accommodation in advance.
- The screenshots advise checking whether an accommodation can be requested.
